What the parameters mean

n is the number of independent trials. p is the probability of Heads on each trial.

Shape of the distribution

The probability of Heads controls which side has the longer tail.

p < 0.5: right-skewed
p = 0.5: symmetric
p > 0.5: left-skewed

Key points

  • The variance is largest when p = 0.5.
  • Increasing n raises both the mean and the variance (when 0 < p < 1).
  • The Bernoulli distribution is the special case n = 1.
